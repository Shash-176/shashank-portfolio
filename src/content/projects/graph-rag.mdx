<Section title="The regression, and the fix">

Adding graph neighbours made one kind of question worse: “which file is the hub?” Neighbour chunks crowded the ranking, and being a hub is not written in any chunk’s text. <Ev id="rag-central">A bonus for in-degree, switched on only for centrality questions, fixed it</Ev>: Recall at 5 went from 0.000 to 1.000 on those questions.

Read that as a fixed bug, not a measured gain: there are three such questions, and the routing keywords match their wording.

</Section>
